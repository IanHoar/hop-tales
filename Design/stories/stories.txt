# Hop Tales stories. scripts/build-stories.py turns this file into
# HopTalesPackage/Sources/Content/Resources/stories.json; don't edit the JSON by hand.
#
# == id | Title | level N | sky weather [| stretch] [| big]
#   stretch  one of the hardest stories at its level, where the next friend's trail treats appear
#   big      the level's big story, read to meet that level's friend
# One sentence per line, ending in . ? or !, with commas and “speech marks” where they belong; they
# show on the path and never reach the matcher. [sky] or [weather] at the start changes the mood
# from that sentence on. *word* is a big word, from a level or two up. ^word is the sentence's new
# word. Keep words free of apostrophes and hyphens: the matcher compares plain words.
#
# Levels follow the UFLI Foundations phonics sequence. Content/Resources/phonics.json lists the
# sounds and heart words each level adds, and the build refuses a word a reader at that level
# can't decode yet. A big word may reach two levels up. Every sentence fits its level's length.
#
# Moments: a [tag] can also name a prop from Content/Resources/props.json to bring into the scene.
#   [bug@bug]      the bug comes in once "bug" has been read, never before, so it can't give the word away
#   [butterfly]    an ambient visitor from the sentence's start; the build refuses one the sentence names
#   [frog*3@frogs] three of them          [kite@kite:sky] a place other than the prop's own
#   [bug@bug:alongside]  runs beside the friend from then on, and dashes off ahead at the end
#   [bed@bed:thought]    shows the prop in a thought bubble over the friend's head for the sentence
# Sky and weather tags (day golden dusk night, clear clouds storm rain snow) are moments too, and
# [clear@sun] waits until "sun" is read. Aim for two or three moments a story. Props leave when
# their sentence ends.
#
# A "# Scene direction:" comment under a story's header is its approved stage direction from the
# Story Desk: what the scene does, line by line, including what the tags can't express yet. It
# covers props, the sky and weather, and companions, never the friend's own poses or gestures.

== meadow-walk | The Meadow Walk | level 1 | day clear
# Scene direction:
#   Bob hops along the path in the sun. A butterfly drifts past.
#   The red bug scurries up and runs alongside Bob from here on.
#   Bob and the bug race along the path together, under gathering clouds.
#   On "wins", the bug dashes ahead and off the right-hand edge of the screen.
Bob can hop.
[butterfly] He can ^hop a lot.
[bug@bug:alongside] He *spots* a red bug.
Bob and the bug run.
[clouds] Bob runs *fast*.
[bee] But the bug wins!

== bob-bug | Bob and the Bug | level 1 | day clouds
# Scene direction:
#   Opens under a cloudy sky.
#   1. Bob hops past a log lying beside the path.
#   2. The red bug from The Meadow Walk comes out and runs beside Bob from here on.
#   4. They hop along together.
#   5. On "sun" the clouds part and the sun comes out.
#   6. A butterfly drifts through the sunshine.
#   7. The light turns golden, and at the end the bug dashes off ahead.
[log@log] Bob hops by the log.
[bug@bug:alongside] Look! The bug is *back*!
“We can hop!” said Bob.
One, two, three, hop!
[clear@sun] Hop, hop, in the sun!
[butterfly] It is fun for Bob!
[golden] “See you!” said the bug.
