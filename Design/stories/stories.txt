# == id | Title | level N | sky weather [| stretch] [| big]
#   stretch  one of the hardest stories at its level, where the next friend's trail treats appear
#   big      the level's big story, read to meet that level's friend
# One sentence per line, ending in . ? or !, with commas and “speech marks” where they belong; they
# show on the path and never reach the matcher. [sky] or [weather] at the start changes the mood
# from that sentence on. *word* is a big word, from a level or two up. ^word is the sentence's new
# word. Keep words free of apostrophes and hyphens: the matcher compares plain words.

== meadow-walk | The Meadow Walk | level 1 | day clear
The hare sat in the sun.
He had a red ^scarf.
He ran up the hill.
[clouds] A bee sat on a bud.
The hare hid in the long *grass*.
Then he ran on and on!

== golden-hour | Golden Hour | level 2 | day clouds
The sun is low and warm.
[golden] The hare hops past the old ^oak.
He stops to smell the *flowers*.
A ^robin sings on the fence.
The fields glow in the gold light.
The hare hops on down the path.

== storm-on-the-hill | Storm on the Hill | level 3 | day clouds
Dark *clouds* roll over the hill.
[storm] The wind shakes the tall grass.
[rain] Big ^drops of rain start to fall.
The hare waits under the oak tree.
[clouds] Soon the storm *drifts* away.
[clear] The sun comes out and the hare hops on.

== moonlight-hop | Moonlight Hop | level 3 | golden clear
[dusk] The sky turns pink as the sun sets.
The hare hops along the ^quiet path.
[night] One by one the stars come out.
The ^moon rises over the meadow.
An owl calls *softly* from the trees.
The *sleepy* hare curls up for the night.

# Level 1 · Bob · short vowels, three-letter words · 3 to 5 words

== bramble-bug | Bob and the Bug | level 1 | day clear
Bob sat on a log.
A red bug ran up.
The bug had six legs.
It sat on his *nose*.
Bob did not *jump*.
The bug hid in moss.

== big-nap | The Big Nap | level 1 | golden clear | stretch
Bob had a big nap.
He sat in the sun.
A fat hen got in.
It sat on his *tail*.
Bob let out a *yelp*!
Then the hen ran off.

== mud-and-jam | Mud and Jam | level 1 | day clouds | stretch
Bob dug in the mud.
Mud got on his leg.
[rain] Then the *rain* fell.
He ran to the den.
Mum had jam buns.
Bob had a *snack*.

# Level 2 · Dash · blends and digraphs · 5 to 7 words

== hare-and-frog | Dash and the Frog | level 2 | day clear
Dash hops up the *grassy* hill.
A green frog sits on a stump.
“Can you swim?” asks the frog.
Then the frog jumps into the pond.
Splash goes the cold water!
Dash laughs and shakes his *whiskers*.

== crunchy-carrot | The Crunchy Carrot | level 2 | day clouds | stretch
Dash digs in the soft black soil.
He finds a thick *orange* carrot.
It is the best carrot yet.
He brings it back to his nest.
Crunch, crunch goes the fresh carrot!
Dash is *stuffed* and has a *snooze*.

== chat-on-the-hill | Chat on the Hill | level 2 | golden clear | stretch
Dash and the thrush sit still.
They chat as the sun slips down.
The thrush sings a *sweet* *little* song.
Dash thumps his feet to the song.
A moth flaps past in the dusk.
Then they rush home to bed.

== hare-big-story | Dash and the Brook | level 2 | day clear | big
Dash hops to the brook.
The brook is fast and wide.
He spots a log across it.
Dash steps on the log with care.
Drip, drop, splash goes the brook below!
Dash jumps off on the far bank.

# Level 3 · Oggy · long vowels, magic e · 6 to 8 words

== puddle-kite | Oggy and the Kite | level 3 | day clear | stretch
Oggy the frog sits by the lake.
A red kite *floats* high in the sky.
The wind takes the kite over the reeds.
It lands on a lily pad near Oggy.
Oggy hops out and grabs the *string*.
He waves as the kite rises up again.

== home-by-the-lake | A Home by the Lake | level 3 | golden clouds | stretch
Oggy wants a home by the lake.
He tries a hole under a *smooth* stone.
It is too cold and much too damp.
He tries a *hollow* reed, but it bends.
At last he finds a wide lily pad.
Oggy makes his home and *yawns* with joy.

== puddle-big-story | The Lily Pad Race | level 3 | day clear | big
The frogs line up on the lily pads.
They will race across the whole lake.
Oggy takes a deep breath and waits.
Then the frogs leap from pad to pad.
Oggy slips, but he gets up and hops.
He is not first, but he smiles.

# Level 4 · Button · vowel teams, two beats · 7 to 9 words

== button-finds-a-button | Button Finds a Button | level 4 | day clear
Button the crow flies over the yellow field.
He sees *something* shine down in the mud.
He swoops down and grabs it with his beak.
It is a round blue button from a coat.
Button keeps it safe in his *hidden* nest.
Now he has seven buttons in a neat row!

== rain-on-the-field | Rain on the Field | level 4 | day clouds
Gray clouds *gather* over the field of corn.
[rain] Soon the rain comes down in cool drops.
Button tucks his head under his black wing.
The farmer runs to the barn with a pail.
[clouds] After the rain, the air smells sweet and clean.
[clear] Button shakes his *feathers* and flies up again.

== scarecrow-coat | The Scarecrow Coat | level 4 | golden clear | stretch
The old scarecrow stands alone in the garden.
His coat has a hole and a missing button.
Button feels sorry for his *lonely* friend.
He brings a shiny button from his nest.
He pokes it into the coat with his beak.
The scarecrow looks proud, and Button *chuckles* happily.

== paper-boat | The Paper Boat | level 4 | day clouds | stretch
A child makes a paper boat by the stream.
The boat floats away down the *rushing* water.
Button sees it spin around a muddy rock.
He flaps down and lifts the boat with care.
He carries it back and sets it down.
The child cheers, and Button bows in the *sunshine*.

== button-big-story | The Harvest Moon | level 4 | dusk clear | big
The harvest moon rises over the golden field.
Button counts the stars as the night grows cool.
A little mouse is lost in the corn.
Button calls to the mouse from a post.
He shows the mouse the way to its burrow.
Then Button flies home to his nest of buttons.

# Level 5 · Marmalade · -ing, -ed and compound words · 8 to 10 words

== marmalade-sunflower | The Tallest Sunflower | level 5 | day clear
Marmalade the cat was napping under the garden bench.
She yawned and *stretched* and looked up at the sky.
A sunflower was reaching higher than the cottage roof.
Marmalade wanted to see the sunflower from the top.
She climbed the fence and jumped onto the *windowsill*.
From there she smiled at the tallest sunflower in town.

== wool-in-the-wind | Wool in the Wind | level 5 | day clouds
Marmalade was playing with a ball of green wool.
The wind picked up and the wool started rolling away.
It bounced along the path and *tangled* around the roses.
Marmalade followed the trail of wool through the garden.
She found the ball resting beside the old *watering* can.
She carried it home and curled up for a rest.

== garden-party | The Garden Party | level 5 | golden clear | stretch
The children were planning a party in the cottage garden.
They hung *lanterns* and baked cupcakes with pink icing.
Marmalade watched everything from the top of the wall.
When the guests arrived, she jumped down to say “hello”.
Everyone laughed as she chased the bubbles across the grass.
It was the most *wonderful* party the garden had seen.

== rainy-afternoon | A Rainy Afternoon | level 5 | day clouds | stretch
[rain] Raindrops were tapping on the window of the cottage.
Marmalade stayed indoors and watched the puddles growing bigger.
She listened to the kettle *whistling* in the kitchen.
Grandma was knitting a scarf beside the crackling fire.
Marmalade pounced on the dangling wool and rolled it away.
Grandma chuckled, and they spent the *afternoon* playing together.

== marmalade-big-story | The Lost Kitten | level 5 | golden clouds | big
One morning, Marmalade heard a tiny “meow” from the hedge.
She searched between the flowerpots and behind the shed.
At last, she discovered a kitten hiding under a bucket.
The kitten was shivering and wanted to find its mother.
Marmalade walked beside it all the way down the lane.
Its mother was waiting, and they purred at the gate.

# Level 6 · Barnacle · longer words, tricky spellings · 9 to 12 words

== nipper-shells | Barnacle and the Shells | level 6 | day clear
Barnacle the crab lives in a rock pool beside the sea.
Every morning, he searches the sand for *beautiful* shells.
Today he found a spiral shell as pink as a sunset!
He carried it carefully because the waves were getting stronger.
He placed it on a flat rock with the other shells.
Now his *collection* shines like treasure when the sun comes out.

== tide-comes-in | When the Tide Comes In | level 6 | golden clouds
Twice every day, the tide comes in and covers the rocks.
Barnacle knows *exactly* when the water will start to rise.
He watches the seaweed begin to float and sway gently.
Tiny fish arrive and dart through the clear salty water.
Barnacle hides in his *favourite* crack until the tide goes out.
Then he climbs out again to see what the sea has left.

== island-adventure | The Island Across the Bay | level 6 | day clear | stretch
Barnacle could see a small island across the sparkling bay.
Did anyone live on the rocks beneath the tall *lighthouse*?
One calm morning, he decided to walk along the sandbar.
The journey was longer than he *expected*, but he kept going.
On the island he met a family of seals sunbathing together.
They told him stories about the ocean until the sun went down.

== nipper-storm | The Night of the Storm | level 6 | dusk clouds | stretch
[storm] The sky turned dark and the wind began to howl.
*Enormous* waves crashed against the rocks and sprayed white foam.
Barnacle held on tightly to the seaweed in his rock pool.
*Although* he was frightened, he remembered the storm would pass.
[rain] Thunder rumbled while the rain poured down for hours.
[clear] By morning, the sea was calm and the beach was *peaceful* again.

== nipper-big-story | The Message in a Bottle | level 6 | day clear | big
One morning, Barnacle noticed a glass bottle wedged between two rocks.
Inside the bottle there was a rolled up piece of paper.
He pulled and twisted until the cork finally popped out!
The message was a drawing of a smiling crab waving hello.
Someone far across the ocean had sent it just for him.
Barnacle kept the drawing and dreamed about his faraway friend.

# Level 7 · Bartholomew · big words, longer sentences · 10 to 14 words

== sprig-tall-grass | Bartholomew in the Tall Grass | level 7 | day clear
Bartholomew the grasshopper lives where the grass grows taller than a house.
Every morning, he climbs to the top of a stem to watch the sunrise.
From up there, the meadow looks like an *enormous* green ocean.
He practises his jumping until he can leap across three stems at once.
The ladybirds cheer whenever he lands without wobbling on the clover.
Bartholomew believes that every day brings a brand new *adventure*.

== sprig-fiddle | The Evening Concert | level 7 | dusk clear
When the sun sets, Bartholomew takes out his tiny wooden fiddle.
The crickets gather beneath the dandelions to listen very quietly.
He plays a gentle *melody* that drifts across the whole meadow.
Even the sleepy bumblebees stop buzzing to hear the music.
The fireflies begin to glow as if they are dancing to the tune.
When the concert ends, everyone *whispers* “thank you” and goes home to sleep.

== sprig-journey | The Journey to the Old Oak | level 7 | golden clouds | stretch
Bartholomew had heard stories about an *ancient* oak at the edge of the meadow.
He packed his leather satchel with clover and set off before breakfast.
The journey was *difficult* because the grass was thick and tangled.
He crossed a trickling stream by balancing carefully on a fallen twig.
By afternoon, he finally reached the enormous tree with its twisted roots.
Resting in its shade, he felt proud of how far he had travelled.

== sprig-rainbow | Chasing the Rainbow | level 7 | day clouds | stretch
[rain] After a heavy shower, a *magnificent* rainbow appeared above the meadow.
Bartholomew was curious to discover where the colourful arch touched the ground.
He hopped and scrambled through puddles that reflected the brightening sky.
Every time he came closer, the rainbow seemed to move further away.
[clear] Eventually the warm sunshine returned and the colours slowly *disappeared*.
Bartholomew laughed because the adventure had been the treasure all along.

== sprig-big-story | The Great Meadow Festival | level 7 | golden clear | big
Once a year, every creature in the meadow gathers for a festival.
The beetles decorate the stems with petals and shimmering spider silk.
Bob brings strawberries and Dash arrives with a basket of carrots.
Oggy and Button organise races while Marmalade and Barnacle tell stories.
When the moon rises, Bartholomew climbs onto a mushroom to play his fiddle.
All his friends dance together, and it is the happiest night of the year!
