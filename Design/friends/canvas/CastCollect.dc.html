The moments, in the order a child meets them
1 · Pick up
The friend lands on the treat; it springs up and flies into a small basket that shows up top-right for a moment.
2 · Big word
A marked harder word, read out loud: a star pops and it's worth 5 steps.
3 · Tally
At The end sign, the basket counts up and the path to the next friend fills.
4 · Basket full
Paper confetti and a wrapped present to open.
5 · Try it on
The present goes straight into the wardrobe, with a Try it on button.
6 · Big story
When the path is full, the next friend waits at The end with a story one level up.
