How it works

Three beats, over and over, until the story is done.

Say the word

Play it on the big screen

AirPlay Hop Tales to your Apple TV. The phone stays in your child's hands and does the listening, while the TV shows the whole world — castle, knight and dragon in one wide view.

For grown-ups

Private by design

Speech is recognised on the device. No audio is recorded or stored, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Made for new readers

Words are set in Andika, a typeface built for literacy, with the same a and g shapes kids learn to write.

Never a wrong answer

Listening is forgiving. If a word gets stuck, Hop Tales reads it aloud — no buzzers, no timers.

No ads, no sign-ups

Nothing to click away to and no account to make. Open it, pick a story, start reading.
