How it works

Three beats, over and over, until the story is done.

  1. 1 Say the word

    A big word sits on the paper card. Your child reads it out loud, and Hop Tales listens right on the device.

  2. 2 Their friend hops

    Heard it! Their reading friend bounds to the next word, unlocking new stories as they progress.

  3. 3 The world changes

    Word by word the story walks on: clouds gather, rain falls, the sun sets and the moon comes up.

For grown-ups

  • Private by design

    Speech is recognised on the device. No audio is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Progress stays on the device, or in your own iCloud or Google account if you choose.

  • Made for new readers

    Big, calm words in a clear storybook typeface, one at a time.

    • Seven levels follow the UFLI Foundations phonics sequence, from short vowels to long words.
    • Every word can be sounded out with the sounds taught so far, plus a few heart words learned by sight.
    • Sentences grow with the reader, from three words to fourteen.

  • Never a wrong answer

    Listening is forgiving. Readers can take all the time they need, and get hints when they're stuck.

  • No ads, no sign-ups

    Nothing to click away to and no account to make. Open it, pick a story, read.

About the developer

Hi, I'm Ian

I've been making iOS apps for about fifteen years, but this is the first one I made for my own kid.

My son was just starting to read, and some nights getting through a page felt like a chore for both of us. He loves games, so I wondered what would happen if every word he read actually made something happen. That turned into a ball, then a bunny, that hops along each time he gets a word right, and waits for him when he doesn't.

It worked better than I hoped. Now he asks to read, and he wants to see where the bunny ends up. I built Hop Tales the way I'd want any app my kid uses to be built: no ads, no accounts, and nothing he says is recorded or leaves the phone. I'm still adding stories, and I hope it helps your reader as much as it's helped mine.
