Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Hop Tales is a read-aloud game for children learning to read, on iPhone, iPad and Android. This policy explains what the Hop Tales app does with information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

The app collects no data

Hop Tales does not collect or share personal information, and nothing you or your child does in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Speech stays on the device. While your child reads, the app uses the device's own speech recognition, set to run on the device only: Apple's on iPhone and iPad, Android's on-device recogniser on Android. The audio is processed on the device and then discarded. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Settings and progress stay on the device, unless you choose to save them to iCloud or your Google account. The child's name or nickname, their accent, help-voice and display settings, and their reading progress are saved on the device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • No accounts of ours. There is nothing to sign up for and no login.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. The app contains no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It never sends anything to us. The only things it exchanges over the internet are with Apple or Google: the optional backup described below, checking and buying Hop Tales Plus through the App Store or Google Play, and, on Android, downloading part of the app's artwork from Google Play.

Saving progress (optional)

During setup, and later in the app's settings, a grown-up can choose to save progress to their own account, so it's there on a new or restored device.

  • On iPhone and iPad, it's your iCloud. The settings and progress are kept in the private iCloud storage of the Apple account signed in on the device, and follow your reader between devices signed in to that account. We can't see it, and it's covered by Apple's privacy policy. "Delete saved progress from iCloud" in the app's settings removes the copy, and you can also manage it in the Settings app under your name, iCloud.
  • On Android, it's your Google account. Android's own backup keeps the settings and progress in the Google account on the device, and restores them when the app is installed on a new device. We can't see it, and it's covered by Google's privacy policy. Turning it off in the app's settings replaces the backed-up copy at Android's next backup.
  • It's off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in the app's settings. Turning it off leaves the progress on the device.
  • No audio is ever saved, to iCloud, Google or anywhere else.

Hop Tales Plus

The optional Hop Tales Plus subscription is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment, and we never see your name, account or payment details. The app only asks the store whether Plus is active on the device, so it knows which stories to open.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for permission to use the microphone (and, on iPhone and iPad, speech recognition) the first time your child reads. It needs them to hear the words being read aloud and move the story along. You can turn them off at any time in the device's settings; the app will still open, but it can't listen while they're off.

Children's privacy

Hop Tales is made for children and is listed in the App Store's Kids Category for ages 6–8. We do not collect any personal information from children. Saving progress is a choice for the grown-up who sets up the app, and it only uses the account already signed in on the device. Hop Tales Plus can only be bought by a grown-up after a parental gate, and the only links out of the app, to these terms and this policy, sit behind the same gate. There is no way for a child to share anything with anyone.

This website

Everything above is about the app. The hoptales.com website sets no cookies; it counts page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookie-free. The promo video on the home page loads from YouTube, in its privacy-enhanced mode, only when you press play; until then nothing is fetched from YouTube.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
